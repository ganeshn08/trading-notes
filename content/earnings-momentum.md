Half-formed idea: post-earnings-announcement drift. Prices appear to keep moving
in the direction of an earnings surprise for weeks after the announcement, which
shouldn't happen in an efficient market.

Open questions before this is worth anything:

- How is "surprise" defined — versus consensus, or versus a time-series model?
- Does it survive costs? Most anomalies don't — see [[backtesting]]
- Is it just a liquidity premium in small caps wearing a costume?
- Indian market specifics: does it hold given the concentration in index names?
